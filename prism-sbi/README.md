## 🔮 Project Vision

**PRISM** transforms traditional banking into an intelligent, proactive, and autonomous financial ecosystem.

Instead of waiting for customers to search for products or request assistance, PRISM continuously understands customer behaviour, predicts future needs, reasons over financial context, simulates multiple strategies, and delivers personalized banking journeys. 

PRISM acts as an **AI-powered Financial Digital Twin** that accompanies every customer throughout their financial lifecycle.

---

## ⚠️ Problem Statement

Current banking systems are largely reactive. Customers receive products only after searching manually, visiting branches, applying themselves, or speaking to advisors.

This results in:
* Missed opportunities & delayed financial guidance
* Poor personalization & generic recommendations
* Low engagement & higher customer acquisition costs
* Lower Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

PRISM introduces an **AI-native intelligence layer** built on a Financial Digital Twin. 

The platform continuously:
1. **Detects** financial signals
2. **Identifies** important life events
3. **Builds** customer hypotheses
4. **Verifies** evidence
5. **Simulates** strategies
6. **Plans** personalized journeys
7. **Executes** approved actions
8. **Learns** continuously

### Core AI Components
* **Financial Digital Twin**
* **Intelligence Agent & Journey Architect**
* **Strategy Simulation Agent**
* **Execution Agent**
* **Governance & Compliance Engine**
* **Learning Engine & Explainability Layer**
